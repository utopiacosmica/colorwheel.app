Why Artists & UI Designers Test Dark Neutrals with White Tint

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the easiest chromatic black to mix for beginners?

French Ultramarine (PB29) + Burnt Umber (PBr7) is the most forgiving studio pairing because both pigments dry quickly in oil, share medium tinting strength, and shift effortlessly between cool blue-gray and warm earth brown without turning green.

How do I export a cohesive dark-mode UI palette from this tool?

Tune your desired cool-slate or warm-obsidian bias and click COPY DARK UI CSS to copy all 6 tonal steps (from deep background masstone to muted mid-gray border tokens) as ready-to-use CSS custom properties.
