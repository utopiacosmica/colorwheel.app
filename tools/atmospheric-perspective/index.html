How Aerial Perspective Changes Color Across Distance Planes

Leonardo da Vinci called atmospheric perspective "the perspective of disappearance." Rather than simply mixing white into distant objects, real optical scattering changes colors in three stages:

Depth Plane Distance Slider Wavelength & Value Behavior
Foreground 0% – 15% Full local saturation, deepest dark shadows, and full warm red/yellow wavelengths.
Near Midground 20% – 45% Yellow and orange warmth begins to soften; darkest shadows lift by 15%–25% toward sky value.
Far Midground / Ridges 50% – 75% Foliage greens shift toward cool blue-green or slate violet; contrast compresses into a narrow mid-high value band.
Distant Horizon 80% – 100% Local color is almost completely replaced by the illuminated atmospheric veil near the horizon sky.
