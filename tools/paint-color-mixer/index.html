Frequently Asked Questions

Why does mixing blue and yellow make gray in RGB instead of green?

Computer and phone displays emit additive RGB light, where pure blue (#0000FF) and yellow (#FFFF00) are mathematical complements that cancel into neutral gray. Physical paint pigments work subtractively: yellow absorbs blue wavelengths and blue absorbs red wavelengths, leaving green wavelengths (500–550nm) as the only light reflected back to your eyes.

How does Kubelka-Munk paint mixing work?

Published by Paul Kubelka and Franz Munk in 1931, the model converts spectral reflectance R(λ) into an absorption-to-scattering ratio K/S = (1 − R)² / (2R) across visible wavelengths. Blending K/S curves proportionally and inverting back to reflectance accurately predicts masstone darkening, chroma shifts, and white tinting strength.

How do I convert a HEX color code into a real paint mixing recipe?

Type or paste any 6-digit HEX code into the Result HEX input field on the right side of the mixer and press Enter or click UNMIX RECIPE. The solver evaluates studio pigment combinations and outputs parts-by-volume along with Colour Index pigment codes.
