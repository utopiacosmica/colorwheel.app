How to Pick Natural Shadow and Highlight Colors

In nature, light and shadow never change brightness alone—they also shift in hue and chroma (saturation):

  • Warm Light / Cool Shadows (Outdoor Sunlight): Direct sunlight warms the lit side of an object toward golden yellow (~55°), while the shadow side is lit by the cool blue dome of the sky, shifting shadows toward ultramarine and blue-violet (~235°).
  • Cool Light / Warm Shadows (Overcast or North Light): Diffused cool sky light shifts highlights toward cool cyan-blue (~205°), causing surrounding warm bounce light to make the shadows appear relatively warm (shifting toward burnt umber/crimson ~18°).
  • Saturation Curve in Shading: Mid-shadows often gain slight chromatic richness before desaturating in deep occlusion crevices, while highlights brighten and gradually desaturate toward the light source color.
