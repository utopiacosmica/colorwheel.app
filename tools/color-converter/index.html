When to Use HSB vs. HSL vs. RGB vs. CMYK

HSB (Hue, Saturation, Brightness) models how artists think about light and pigment intensity, where 100% brightness + 100% saturation yields the purest hue. HSL (Hue, Saturation, Lightness) places pure white at 100% lightness and pure hue at 50% lightness for CSS styling. HEX and RGB define additive screen primaries for web and iOS/macOS development, while CMYK estimates subtractive cyan, magenta, yellow, and black ink percentages for print.
