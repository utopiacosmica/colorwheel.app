HEX Color Editor – Modify Hue, Saturation, Brightness, RGB & Alpha

Fine-tune any HEX color code in a distraction-free, full-screen preview. Paste a 6-digit or 8-digit HEX code in the top-right box and adjust its Hue, Saturation, Brightness, individual Red, Green, and Blue channels, or Opacity simultaneously.

How to Lighten, Darken, or Desaturate a HEX Color

Raw hexadecimal codes (such as #FA75D4) mix red, green, and blue light, making it difficult to make a color slightly warmer, softer, or darker by editing the digits manually. Our synchronized HSB and RGB sliders let you shift brightness and saturation independently while keeping your exact hue intact.
