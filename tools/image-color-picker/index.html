How Perceptual Oklab Palette Extraction Works

Standard image palette generators cluster raw RGB numbers, often missing small high-chroma focal accents or merging distinct shadow tones. This tool converts sampled image pixels into perceptual Oklab space, runs iterative k-means clustering, and sorts your extracted palette from deepest shadow accent to brightest highlight so you immediately read the image’s value key.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I pick a HEX color code from an image?

Click UPLOAD PHOTO (or drag-and-drop / paste any PNG, JPG, or WebP image), move your cursor over the picture to preview colors, and click any spot to lock the crosshair and copy its HEX code.

Are my uploaded photos private?

Yes—every calculation happens directly inside your device’s browser memory via HTML5 Canvas. No image data is ever uploaded or stored externally.

Why do single-pixel eyedroppers mislead painters?

Camera sensors and JPEG compression scatter warm and cool noise pixels inside seemingly flat areas. Sliding Sample Brush Average to 5px–11px blends those micro-speckles in linear light, giving you clean, mixable mass color.
