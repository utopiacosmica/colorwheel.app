How the 4-Color Zorn Palette Works Without Blue

Swedish portrait painter Anders Zorn (1860–1920) achieved breathtaking realism with just four tubes of paint by treating Ivory Black as a very dark, desaturated blue:

Zorn Pigment Color Wheel Role Key Mixtures on the Palette
Yellow Ochre (#CC8E35) Warm Yellow Primary Mix with White for warm highlights; mix with Ivory Black for olive and sage greens.
Vermilion / Cad Red (#E03628) Warm Red Primary Mix with Ochre and White for luminous portrait skin tones; mix with Black for deep umber/burgundy.
Ivory Black (#1B2228) Cool Blue Substitute Mix with Titanium White to create cool Payne's Gray and slate-blue sky tones.
Titanium White (#FBFBF9) Value Lightener & Cooler Raises value and reveals the cool blue undertone of Ivory Black and the rose undertone of Red.
