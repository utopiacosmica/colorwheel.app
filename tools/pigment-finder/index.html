Why Single-Pigment Codes Matter More Than Paint Brand Names

Paint manufacturers often invent poetic names like “ Venetian Sunset ” or “ Pacific Mist ” that blend three or four pigments together—causing muddy mixes on the palette. Checking the Colour Index code printed on the back of a tube guarantees clean, predictable mixing behavior, known opacity, and verified ASTM lightfastness.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are Colour Index (CI) pigment codes on paint tubes?

Colour Index International codes standardize chemical pigments worldwide: PW (White), PY (Yellow), PO (Orange), PR (Red), PV (Violet), PB (Blue), PG (Green), PBr (Brown), and PBk (Black). Two brands using pure PB29 contain the exact same sodium aluminosilicate Ultramarine pigment.

How accurate are HEX-to-RAL and HEX-to-pigment matches?

This tool computes perceptual distance in Oklab color space so brightness, hue, and chroma match how the human eye judges physical swatches under D65 daylight. Always verify final industrial coatings with a physical RAL fan deck and test paint undertones with a white drawdown.

Can I search backward from a pigment or RAL code to get its HEX?

Yes—enter codes such as PR108, PB15:3, RAL 6005, or names like Cerulean in the top-left search box and click FIND CODE.
