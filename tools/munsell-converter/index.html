Painters, realist ateliers, industrial designers, and colorists rely on Albert Munsell's three-dimensional color space because it separates perceptual lightness (Value) from pigment intensity (Chroma) across 10 balanced hue families.

How the 10 Munsell Hue Families Map to the Color Wheel

Unlike standard RGB wheels with 3 primaries, the Munsell system divides the hue circle into 5 Principal Hues and 5 Intermediate Hues, each graded from 2.5 to 10 (with 5 representing the pure center of each family):

Munsell Code Hue Family Name Representative Artist Pigments
R (5R) Red Cadmium Red Medium, Pyrrole Red, Naphthol Red
YR (5YR) Yellow-Red (Orange) Cadmium Orange, Burnt Sienna, Raw Sienna
Y (5Y) Yellow Cadmium Yellow Light, Yellow Ochre, Hansa Yellow
GY (5GY) Green-Yellow Sap Green, Terre Verte, Chromium Oxide Green
G & BG Green & Blue-Green Viridian, Phthalo Green, Cobalt Teal
B & PB Blue & Purple-Blue Cerulean Blue (B), Ultramarine Blue (PB), Cobalt Blue
P & RP Purple & Red-Purple Dioxazine Purple (P), Quinacridone Magenta, Alizarin Crimson (RP)
