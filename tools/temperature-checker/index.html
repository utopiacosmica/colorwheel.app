Absolute vs. Relative Color Temperature in Painting & Design

Artists and designers use two layers of color temperature:

  • Absolute Temperature: Divides the color wheel into the Warm hemisphere (Reds, Oranges, Yellows, peaking near Red-Orange at ~15°) and the Cool hemisphere (Greens, Cyans, Blues, Violets, peaking near Cyan-Blue at ~195°).
  • Relative Undertone (Hue Bias): Compares a color to its immediate neighbors within the same hue family. For example, Lemon Yellow is a cool yellow because it leans slightly toward green, whereas Cadmium Yellow Deep is a warm yellow because it leans toward orange.
